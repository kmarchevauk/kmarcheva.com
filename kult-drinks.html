KULT Drinks

Project type

  • Branding
  • Packaging
  • Campaign Rollout

KULT turns fermented cabbage juice into a bold lifestyle brand that celebrates its unusual origins and links a healthy gut with a good mood.

The brief

Create a functional drinks brand centred on emotional wellbeing. The gut-brain connection, and how gut health can influence mood, became the starting point. As an unusual product, it needed to feel approachable, spark curiosity on the shelf and avoid health-aisle clichés.

The concept

The cabbage became the hero. Its folds and layers form a pattern wrapping the can, cut through by a bold vertical wordmark. The name nods to live cultures and cult-favourite brands, and the tagline “that gut feeling” ties gut health to intuition.

The outcome

The visual language is confident and a little playful, with expressive pattern, punchy colour and a tone of voice that owns its quirks. I explored how it could live across packaging and print and digital campaigns. The project taught me to embrace the “good weird”, because unconventional choices often spark the most curiosity.
