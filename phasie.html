Phasie

Project type

  • UX/UI Design
  • Branding

Phasie is a strength training app that understands how women’s energy and hormones change through the month. It stays supportive on the harder weeks and keeps progress motivating on every one.

The brief

Create a training app tailored to women, with progress results that reflect how their bodies really change. It had to be inclusive of every woman, including those with irregular cycles or going through menopause.

The concept

Most training plans treat strength as steady from day to day, but women’s hormones rise and fall across the month. Sport science research is mostly built on men, and only 6% of studies focus on women alone. Phasie compares each lift with the same point in the previous cycle.

The outcome

The result is an app that helps women train with their bodies through the month. It explains why some weeks feel harder, suggests small changes like longer rests and highlights each person’s strongest weeks from their own data. The visual language is calm and confident, with a deep plum background, Moret for big moments and a clear role for each colour. Cherry marks actions, lilac the body and apricot every win.

Source for the 6% is Cowley et al. 2021, Women in Sport and Physical Activity Journal.
