Hi, I’m Kristiyana, a multidisciplinary designer based in Nottingham.

About me

With a background in graphic design and UX/UI, I’m interested in the ways ideas can take shape through visual language and thoughtful experiences.

I’ve always been fascinated by how design communicates, tells stories and influences the way we see and interact with the world. I enjoy developing concepts, experimenting with typography and imagery, and finding creative solutions to design challenges. For me, the most interesting work is built around a strong idea, where every visual decision has a purpose.

My work spans branding, visual identity, packaging, print and digital design, including web and interface design. I like working across different formats and seeing how a visual concept can translate into a consistent experience, whether it’s a brand, a product or a digital interface. I bring curiosity, attention to detail and an open-minded approach to collaboration, always valuing the balance between creative expression and functionality.

Outside of design, I love travelling, discovering new places, collecting perfumes and finding inspiration in art, culture and everyday things. I’m always keen to learn and try new creative directions.
